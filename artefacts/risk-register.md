# Risk register & threat model

**What it is:** A living list of risks (delivery, technical, security, privacy,
operational), each with a likelihood, an impact, a mitigation and a status. It
includes a lightweight threat model.

## Contents
1. Risk table: ID (`RISK-nn`), description, likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner, status
2. Threat model: assets, entry points and trust boundaries, and threats listed per STRIDE category, with mitigations
3. Data inventory: the personal or sensitive data handled, where it is stored, how long it is kept

See the [security guide](../guides/security.md).
