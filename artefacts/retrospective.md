# Retrospective

**What it is:** A short reflection after a release or at a quarterly
checkpoint, covering the product and the process.

## Contents
1. What went well
2. What went badly
3. What surprised me (including agent behaviour)
4. Actions, each routed to: the product backlog, `AGENTS.md`, or a process change proposal

Agents can draft a retrospective from PR history and gate records. The Owner edits and owns it.
