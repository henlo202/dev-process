# Initiative brief

**What it is:** A one-to-two page framing of the initiative: the problem, who it
is for, what success looks like, and what is out of scope.

**Why it matters:** This is the root of all traceability. Agents read it to
understand intent when requirements are silent.

## Contents
1. Problem statement
2. Target users and their needs
3. Goals and measurable success criteria
4. Scope and **non-goals**
5. Constraints (time, budget, technology, legal)
6. Risk tier, with justification
7. Open questions and assumptions

## Rules
- Keep it short. If it grows past two pages, the detail belongs in requirements or design.
- Update it when the goal changes, and record the change in the document's history section.
